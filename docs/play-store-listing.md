## Full Description (4000 character limit)

**Lala — Local Area Logging Application**

When something happens, memory alone isn't enough. Lala captures a timestamped, GPS-tagged log of events as they happen — with photo and video attached — so you can always look back at exactly what happened, when, and where.

**CAPTURE THE FULL PICTURE**
Every entry is automatically stamped with:
• GPS location — where it happened
• Date and time — when it happened
• Photos and video — what happened
• Your written notes — the details that matter

**BUILT FOR REAL SITUATIONS**
Lala is made for anyone who needs a reliable event log:
• Neighbor disputes — noise, property lines, pets, harassment
• Landlord & tenant issues — move-in/move-out condition, repairs, deposit disputes
• Workplace incidents — log events as they occur
• Insurance documentation — damage, accidents, property condition
• Co-parenting & custody — keep a dated log of events
• HOA and property disputes — photos with time and location
• General event logging — anything you may need to reference later

**A TIMELINE YOU CAN TRUST**
Your events are organized chronologically, creating a running history you can scroll back through. A pattern of dated, located, photographed events tells a far stronger story than memory alone.

**YOUR DATA STAYS YOURS**
Lala stores your event log privately on your device. No account required to start logging. You decide what to keep, what to export, and who sees it.

**SIMPLE BY DESIGN**
Something happens → open Lala → snap a photo or video → add a note → done. The GPS coordinates, date, and time are captured automatically. No setup, no friction, no missed moments.

**WHY "LALA"?**
Lala stands for Local Area Logging Application — a simple tool with one job: keeping an accurate, organized log of the events that happen around you.

Download Lala today and never rely on memory again.

## Listing Notes (not part of the store copy)

1. **Wording choice:** The copy now uses "event" and "log" throughout instead of "record," "proof," or "documentation." This reads softer and more neutral — closer to a life-event logger than a legal tool — and steers well clear of any implied legal claims.
2. **Google Play keyword indexing:** Play indexes your title, short description, and full description. The highest-value terms are in the title/short description; the long description reinforces them with natural repetition (aim ~3–5 mentions of core terms, which this draft hits).
3. **Feature graphic & screenshots** convert more than text. Show: (1) an event entry with visible GPS/time stamp, (2) the timeline view, (3) camera capture in action. Caption each screenshot with a keyword phrase.
4. **Category suggestion:** Tools or Productivity. Avoid Lifestyle — wrong shelf for this positioning.
5. **The "tattle tale" angle** is intentionally absent. Neutral event-logging framing converts better and avoids sounding petty.
