# Lala — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: July 2026_

Lala ("the app") is a local event-logging application published by Hardline Forge. This policy explains what data the app handles and how.

## The short version

**Everything you log stays on your device.** Lala has no accounts, no servers, and no analytics. We cannot see your entries, photos, videos, or location — ever.

## Data the app collects and where it goes

| Data | Why it's used | Where it's stored | Shared? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Location (GPS) | To tag log entries with where they happened | On your device only | No |
| Photos & videos you capture | Attached to your log entries | On your device only (app-private storage) | No |
| Microphone audio | Recorded as part of videos you capture | On your device only | No |
| Text you enter (titles, comments, tags) | The content of your log | On your device only | No |
| Diagnostic log | A local record of app activity to help debug problems | On your device only | Only if you explicitly share it |

## What the app does NOT do

- No account creation or sign-in
- No data transmitted to us or any third party
- No analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs
- No access to your existing photo library or files — Lala only handles the photos and videos it captures itself

## Network access

Lala's map view downloads map tiles from OpenStreetMap servers. This is the app's only network use. Tile requests reveal your approximate viewing area to the tile server (as any map app does), but none of your logged data is sent.

## Exports and sharing

PDF reports, filmstrip images, and the diagnostic log leave your device **only when you export or share them yourself**. You choose what to export and who receives it.

## Data deletion

Deleting an entry deletes its attached media. Uninstalling the app deletes all app data. There is nothing to delete on our side, because we never had it.

## Permissions requested

- **Camera** — capture photos/videos for entries
- **Microphone** — audio for captured videos
- **Location** — GPS-tag entries
- **Network** — map tiles only

## Children

Lala is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone.
